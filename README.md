
Retail Bank Credit Risk & Portfolio Analysis | Power BI, DAX, Power Query
* Cleaned and standardized raw retail banking data (1,500+ loan records across 4 regions) in Power Query by resolving casing inconsistencies, whitespace anomalies, and missing occupation categories.
* Engineered custom DAX measures and calculated columns (Default Rate, Default Loan Amount, FICO Credit Tiers) to evaluate a ₹2.07B loan portfolio with an average FICO score of 689.4 and 13.67% interest rate.
* Designed an interactive executive dashboard identifying that Home loans drive peak portfolio volume while Business applicants carry the highest default risk (~5.7% vs. 3.33% overall average).
